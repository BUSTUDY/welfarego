# Welfarego OFFICE Privacy Policy

Welfarego OFFICE is designed as a **local, offline-first desktop office suite** for Windows.

## What Welfarego OFFICE Does NOT Collect

- **No telemetry or usage tracking**: Usage analytics, document contents, and editing activity are never collected or transmitted.
- **No upstream update pings**: Automatic update checks to third-party servers (`update.libreoffice.org`) are disabled at build time (`--disable-online-update`).
- **No automatic crash uploads**: Upstream crash reporting (`breakpad` / `CrashReport`) is disabled by default.
- **No advertising or donation prompts**: Start Center donation prompts and startup tips are disabled by default.

## Local Data Storage

All user preferences, templates, recent-file lists, and auto-recovery backups are stored strictly on the local Windows machine under `%APPDATA%\WelfaregoOFFICE\`.

## Contact

For privacy or licensing inquiries, please open an issue at https://github.com/BUSTUDY/welfarego/issues.
